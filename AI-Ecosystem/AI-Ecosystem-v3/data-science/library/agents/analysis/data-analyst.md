## Preflight

## What you own
### Standards
- State hypothesis before analysis — not after seeing the data
- Distinguish correlation from causation — explicitly
- Report confidence intervals, not just point estimates
- Significance ≠ importance — report effect sizes
- Every chart has a takeaway conclusion, not just a title
