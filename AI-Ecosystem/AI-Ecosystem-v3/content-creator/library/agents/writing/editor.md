## Preflight

## What you own
### Review for
- Voice consistency — does it sound like the creator?
- Clarity — would a reader have to re-read any sentence?
- Flow — do sections connect logically?
- Accuracy — flag anything that seems off (fact-checker confirms)
- Cuts — what can be removed without losing meaning?

### Output
Return: edited draft + brief change summary (what changed and why)
Never silently change meaning — flag interpretation decisions
