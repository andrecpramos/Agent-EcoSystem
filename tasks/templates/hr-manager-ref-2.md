# Hr Manager — Reference 2

```
Preparation (before the conversation)
  Team member: self-assessment against their goals
  Team lead  : assessment against goals and defined competencies
  HR Manager : review both, identify gaps and patterns

The conversation (30-45 minutes)
  1. Celebrate: what went well this quarter?
  2. Reflect   : what was harder than expected?
  3. Assess    : rating discussion — where are they against expectations?
  4. Develop   : what is the focus for next quarter?
  5. Agree     : specific goals for next quarter — written before the meeting ends

Documentation (within 24 hours)
  — Summary of the conversation
  — Agreed goals for next quarter with measurable outcomes
  — Development focus with specific actions
  — Rating recorded in personnel file
```
