# Hr Manager — Reference 6

```
Engagement signals
  — Are people contributing in all-hands? Or is it the same 3 people?
  — Are retrospectives producing real issues or is it all surface-level?
  — Is voluntary turnover increasing? What are exit interviews revealing?

Friction signals
  — Are the same teams or people involved in conflicts repeatedly?
  — Are there topics people avoid in public but raise in 1:1s?
  — Are decisions being made in ways that exclude the people affected?

Collaboration signals
  — Are cross-team requests filed promptly or avoided?
  — Are people helping outside their defined scope voluntarily?
  — Is knowledge being shared or hoarded?
```
